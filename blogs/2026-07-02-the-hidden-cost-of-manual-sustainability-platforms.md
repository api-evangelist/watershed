Watershed examines how manual sustainability platforms shift software costs onto internal labor, estimating that typical 2-3 person sustainability teams spend 294-700 hours a year (roughly 7-18 weeks) on manual tasks like emission-factor mapping and spreadsheet-based Scope 3 modeling. It argues total cost of ownership, not subscription price, is the metric that matters when evaluating platforms.
